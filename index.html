About Agro Scen Gh Ltd (ASGL)

Transforming Raw Agricultural Waste Into High-Value Economic Wealth

Agro Scen Gh Ltd (ASGL) is a pioneering Ghanaian climate-smart agribusiness headquartered in Sunyani West, Bono Region. We address key agricultural vulnerabilities by creating a completely closed-loop circular model.

We produce FDA-tested high-value organic cereal mixes (Nourish 10 / Cereal-10 Weanimix), convert agro-industrial cassava peel waste into rich animal feed, and generate proprietary bio-fertilizers that restore soil vitality across West Africa.

Agricultural Crisis in Ghana

Bottlenecks Threatening Ghana's Food Security

Conventional agribusiness in Ghana faces systemic pressures, high input costs, and massive post-harvest waste.

Severe Impact

Smallholder farmers suffer from skyrocketing synthetic fertilizer and commercial feed prices.

Soil Fertility Loss & Chemical Degradation

Over-reliance on synthetic fertilizers depletes organic soil nutrients over time, leading to lower crop yields and land degradation across regions.

40% Post-Harvest Losses

Inadequate processing, poor storage, and unutilized agricultural residues lead to up to 40% loss of total seasonal produce.

Skyrocketing Input Costs (60-70%)

Animal feed and synthetic fertilizer inputs account for 60% to 70% of total operational burden for livestock and crop farmers.

Fluctuating Raw Commodity Prices

Price instability in raw grain markets severely impacts farmer margins without value-added local processing capacity.
