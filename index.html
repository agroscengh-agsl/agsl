Agricultural Crisis

Critical Pain Points in Ghana's Agriculture

Chemical overuse, soaring input costs, and unprocessed crop losses severely restrict farmer incomes and threaten regional food security.

Soil Fertility Loss

Decades of synthetic chemical overuse have depleted natural soil biome across Ghanaian farmlands, reducing long-term yields and threatening harvest security.

Depleted Topsoil Biome

High Input Costs (60-70%)

Smallholders face an extreme economic burden due to skyrocketing commercial feed, imported fertilizer prices, and third-party markup loops.

60%-70% Income Erosion

40% Post-Harvest Loss

Selling crops raw due to lack of local processing infrastructure causes massive post-harvest spoilage and missed value-addition opportunities.

High Raw Produce Spoilage

Nutritional Food Gap

Consumers and families lack reliable access to affordable, high-nutrition, locally processed organic cereals, creating malnutrition vulnerabilities.

Limited Local Smart Foods
Volatiles & Price Shocks

Volatile Commodity & Import Pricing

Smallholder farmers are trapped in severe price fluctuations for raw maize and imported feeds. Agro Scen Gh Ltd's circular feed protection decouples operations from foreign market shocks.

The Innovation

The Zero-Waste Circular Solution

An integrated cyclical ecosystem converting agricultural byproducts into valuable organic feeds, fertilizers, and nutritious cereal mixes.

01

Sourcing Split

60% produced on our own fields (2,400 kg), 40% from contracted outgrower partners (1,600 kg).

02

Smart Processing

Cereals processed into 2,600 kg high-nutrient multi-grain mixes (Weanimix / Cereal-10 Smart Mix).

03

Zero-Waste Loop

1,400 kg processing residues bio-converted into livestock satiation feed bases.

04

Livestock Satiation

Sustains 700+ livestock (goats, poultry, swine) with high-protein circular feed.

05

Bio-Fertilization

Animal manure converted into organic bio-pesticide and soil restorer to reboot grain yields.
