The Agricultural Crisis

Severe Inefficiencies Bottlenecking Smallholders & Markets

Sub-Saharan agricultural systems face structural crises across soil health, waste management, and input costs.

Soil Degradation

Heavy synthetic fertilizer overuse has severely depleted organic soil matter, reducing crop yield efficiency year-over-year.

40% Post-Harvest Loss

Millions of tons of cassava peels, crop residues, and biomass rot in fields, creating environmental hazards and financial loss.

Escalating Feed Costs

Conventional livestock feeds (maize, soy) have skyrocketed in cost, pricing local poultry and livestock farmers out of profitability.

Extreme Price Volatility

Import dependency exposes Ghanaian agri-businesses to global supply shocks, currency devaluation, and unreliable supply chains.

2

3

4
